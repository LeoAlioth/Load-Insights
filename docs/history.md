## Edge scale experiment, 2026-10-09


The last open item of the 2026-10-09 ponytail ledger (the other six were fixed and shipped): `EDGE_SCALE_REL = 0.02`
in Load_Insights `insights/detect.py` - a large step's measurement error as a fixed 2 % of its size in `edge_scale()`.
